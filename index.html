About me

Picked ECE since hardware just made sense to me. Started out with the basics — microcontrollers, simple circuits, just figuring out how stuff actually works. Then I got a Vaaman board and it got me really hooked. Like building hardware itself. FPGAs are super niche, but that's the point.

Been looking at more general ECE stuff lately — digital design, communication, control systems, analog. Analog is brutal. It never behaves how you want it to, and it takes forever to get a real feel for it. But you gotta understand it if you want to work with real hardware and not just theory.

Not entirely sure what's next. Just want to keep working close to the metal — FPGAs, embedded. For now, just working on whatever is hard enough to keep me busy.

Now

Hardware & FPGAs

I recently got my hands on the Soan Papdi board—an iCE40UP5K FPGA-based board designed by Hardik Seth, who was kind enough to send one my way. I've been spending time diving into the open-source toolchain, running through the example designs, and wrapping my head around how the board works end-to-end before writing my own bitstreams. You can check out the examples repository here (and read more about the board here).

CORDIC & ASIC Design

Working on a CORDIC implementation right now, rotation and vectoring mode. It's a fun, self-contained problem that's really making me think in hardware terms about maths. It's actually got me seriously considering picking up ASIC design next—CORDIC feels like the right thing to start with.

PCB Design

Continuing my journey into PCB design. I already finished a custom macropad, and right now I'm actively working on a music player PCB.

ESP32-S3 (N8R8) based music player PCB. Audio out through PCM5102 DAC and TPA amplifier, high-speed SD card in 4-bit mode for fast reads, and a proper BMS with battery protection. Currently in progress.
